Both the UTC timeline and card explicitly label `07:52 KST (22:52Z Nov 23)` so there is zero silent date shifting.

Lead #3 explicitly flags `(unverified IRGC claim)` and centers the verified market settlement ($91.40/bbl) and verified Reuters reporting.

On **Tuesday, 24 November**, Huang held his all-hands apology and NVDA fell **5.86% to $155.38**. Our ticker and numbers strip strictly report **Tuesday's -5.86% / $155.38** move, explicitly dating Monday's +31.48% surge as `(Nov 23)`.

Using `2 killed, 40+ injured` accurately reflects verified 24 November reporting without citing unverified peaks.
